{%- comment -%} The hero's cover, as an image the browser sizes: a phone takes the 960 or 1280px copy (script/cover-variants.sh) instead of the 1920px original, and a browser that reads WebP takes the WebP of that size, about half the bytes. As a CSS background it had one size, which made it a phone's slowest paint. It is the first thing on the page: eager, and fetched ahead of everything else. `sizes` is the width it is drawn at, not the box's: object-fit: cover fills a box taller than the image's shape, so the cover is drawn wider than the screen. Each caller passes its own: the home banner is 3.2:1 in a tall box, a post's 1.9:1 in a box whose height follows the title. Decorative, as the background was: the credit under the hero names it.
